# Viewing a Data2Science Dashboard

## What this is

The D2SB creates a small website that lives in a shared SharePoint folder. Set it up once on your computer (about 5 minutes), and every time we publish an update it appears on your machine automatically.

You open the reports in your web browser like any website, but the files come from your own computer, so there's no login or link to remember. This helps us to restrict the viewing of all reports to those on a study team.

## One-time setup

You need the OneDrive app signed in with your University account.

1. Open the study folder link we sent you in your browser.
2. In the toolbar above the file list, click **Add shortcut to My files**. (If you see **Sync** instead, click that.)
3. Wait a minute, then find the folder on your computer:
   - **Mac:** in Finder, under **OneDrive - University of Iowa** in the sidebar.
   - **Windows:** in File Explorer, under **OneDrive - University of Iowa**.
4. Right-click the folder and choose **Always Keep on This Device**. This downloads all the report files, so pages open quickly and look right, even offline.

## Opening the reports

1. In the study folder on your computer, double-click **index.html**. It opens in your web browser as the reports' home page.
2. Bookmark that page (Cmd+D on a Mac, Ctrl+D on Windows) so next time you can open it from your browser.
3. Use the links and menus on the pages to move between reports.

Open the reports from the folder on your computer. Clicking **index.html** on the SharePoint website or in Teams shows a preview or downloads the file instead of the reports.

## Getting updates

When we publish new or revised reports, OneDrive (if running) copies them to your computer, usually within a few minutes, as long as your computer is on and connected.

If you already have a report open, refresh the page (Cmd+R on a Mac, F5 on Windows) to see the latest version.

## Rules for using the reports

- **Look, don't change.** Don't edit, move, rename or delete files in the folder. Any change would sync to everyone on the team. Your access is normally view-only, which prevents this. If we share a manuscript draft for editing online, the site links to it, and you edit it there in Word, not in this folder.
- **Keep the reports within the study team.** Don't forward, email, upload or copy the files to anyone outside the team or to other locations. Results may come from licensed data, such as MarketScan, whose agreement limits who can see them.
- **Ask before you publish.** Before using a figure or number in a paper, poster, grant or talk, check with us that it's final and cleared for sharing.
- **Tell us when someone joins or leaves the team,** so we can update who has access to the folder.

## If something isn't working

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Pages look plain or unstyled, or images are missing | The files haven't finished downloading. Right-click the study folder, choose **Always Keep on This Device**, wait for the OneDrive icon to show it's up to date, then refresh. |
| A report we mentioned isn't there yet | Check that OneDrive is running and signed in (cloud icon in the menu bar or taskbar), wait a few minutes, then refresh. |
| **Add shortcut to My files** and **Sync** are both missing or greyed out | You may not have access yet. Contact us. |
| You deleted or changed a file by mistake | Tell us right away. We can restore it and republish. |
